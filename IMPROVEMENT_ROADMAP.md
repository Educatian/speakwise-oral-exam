# Improvement Roadmap

## Immediate Product Quality Goals

### Student Trust

- Make the interview screen calmer and easier to follow.
- Reduce pressure during silence and recording states.
- Present results as evidence plus next steps, not just a score.

### Instructor Clarity

- Show course-level signals that help instructors act quickly.
- Highlight where submissions are weak, sparse, or inconsistent.
- Support template-driven course creation and repeatable deployment.

### Institution Readiness

- Keep branding, permissions, and course visibility scoped by institution.
- Align onboarding and reporting with institutional workflows.
- Build toward auditability and governance rather than only feature breadth.

## Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Trust and Transparency

- Calmer landing and onboarding experience
- Evidence-based results summary
- More supportive live interview status language
- Cleaner instructor dashboard structure

### Phase 2: Instructor Productivity

- Course templates and duplication
- Cohort-level insight summaries
- Submission triage states
- Export-ready reporting views

### Phase 3: Institutional Operations

- Institution branding controls
- Role-aware dashboards
- Review and override workflows
- Audit logs and deployment metrics

## Review Checklist

Before shipping a new UI feature, check:

- Does it reduce ambiguity?
- Does it reduce pressure for students?
- Does it make evaluation more explainable?
- Does it help instructors act faster?
- Does it fit institution-level deployment, not just a single-user demo?
